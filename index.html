RGCSM Academic Framework, Examination & Grading System

📝

Two Processes of Registration

1. Offline Registration:

Fill the application form provided with the student prospectus at our Khair study centre. Submit attested photocopies of your last educational qualification, 2 recent passport-size photos, and Category Certificate (ST/SC/Handicapped) for fee relaxation. An official Student ID Card is issued by RGCSM H.O.

2. Online Registration:

Registered candidates can apply online. The Center Director logs into the RGCSM center portal, uploads required documents with candidate photograph, and processes payment. A permanent RGCSM Enrollment Number is generated for dispatching the student kit bag, notebook, marksheets, and diploma.

💻

On-Demand Online Examination System

  • Continuous On-Demand Schedule: Examinations conducted all round the year at our authorized RGCSM center in Khair.
  • Theory Question Paper: Objective type format with 50 questions per module (2 marks each = 100 Marks total per paper). Duration: 30 Minutes per paper.
  • Practical & Viva: Total 100 Marks (60 Marks for Lab Practice & 40 Marks for Viva Voce).
  • Passing Criteria: Minimum qualifying marks for semester exam is 40% for both Diploma and Certificate courses.
  • Result & Marksheet: Issued within 30 days of online examination by RGCSM Head Office.

Frequently Asked Questions on RGCSM Diplomas & Saurya Computer Education Khair
Q1 What is Saurya Computer Education's affiliation and registration status?
A1 Saurya Computer Education in Khair is an Authorised Study Centre and franchise of Rajeev Gandhi Computer Saksharta Mission (RGCSM). RGCSM is an autonomous national organization registered under the Societies & Public Trust Act 1860, ISO 9001:2015 certified, and registered under the C.R. Act of Ministry of HRD (Dept. of Secondary & Higher Education), Govt. of India. Our center is located at Taintigaon Tiraha, near KIC (Kedar Inter College), opposite Central Bank in Khair, Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh.
Q2 How does the admission registration process work (Offline vs Online)?
A2 Students can register in two ways:
1. Offline Registration: Visit our study centre near KIC Khair, obtain the prospectus, fill the application form, and submit 2 recent passport photos, attested educational certificates, and category certificate (if claiming SC/ST fee relaxation). A verified Student ID Card will be issued.
2. Online Registration: Apply online through this portal. Our Center Director submits candidate credentials via the RGCSM authorized center panel on rgcsm.org, generating an official enrollment number used to dispatch your student kit, marksheet, and diploma.
Q3 Are course materials and kit bags provided free of cost with admissions?
A3 Yes! All enrolled diploma students receive free-of-cost written course material for theory classes, an official student kit bag, and notebook directly through our RGCSM study centre.
Q4 What are the fee structures and concessions available for students?
A4 Our fee structure is completely transparent and matches official prospectus standards:
• 1-Year Flagship Diplomas (DOAP - Code R20111, ADCA - Code R20118): ₹15,000 for General Candidates, with a special fee concession of ₹14,000 for SC/ST/Handicapped candidates.
• 6-Month Diploma (DCA - Code R10104): ₹8,000 for General Candidates, and ₹7,500 for SC/ST/Handicapped candidates.
• DCAA (Computer Application & Accounting): ₹9,000.
• Tally Prime with GST: ₹5,500.
• RG-CIT / CCOAI (CCC Equivalent): ₹3,500.
• Python Programming: ₹6,500.
Flexible installment payment options are available for all diploma courses.
Q5 What is the examination procedure and qualifying criteria?
A5 RGCSM conducts an On-Demand Online Examination system all round the year:
• Theory: 50 objective multiple-choice questions (2 marks each = 100 marks total per paper, 30 minutes duration) delivered on screen.
• Practical Exam: 100 Marks (60 Marks for Lab Practical Exercise + 40 Marks for Viva Voce).
• Passing Standard: Minimum qualifying score is 40% in each semester examination. Re-examination is available if a student fails to clear a module.
Q6 How does the RGCSM percentage grading scale work?
A6 Grades are assigned based on total marks obtained across theory and practical modules:
• >= 80%: Grade A+ (Outstanding / Distinction)
• 70% to < 80%: Grade A (Excellent)
• 60% to < 70%: Grade B+ (Very Good)
• 50% to < 60%: Grade B (Good)
• 40% to < 50%: Grade C (Satisfactory / Pass)
• < 40%: Re-Examination Required
Q7 Are RGCSM diploma certificates valid for government employment exchanges?
A7 Yes, absolutely. After successful course completion, Certificate / Diploma will be awarded by Rajeev Gandhi Computer Saksharta Mission (H.O. Kota), and these certificates/diplomas will be valid for registering the students in different employment exchanges all over India. Certificates and marksheets are verifiable anytime under the "Student Section" on www.rgcsm.org.
